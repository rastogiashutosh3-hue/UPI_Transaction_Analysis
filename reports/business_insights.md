### Merchant Category Analysis

- Shopping generated the highest transaction value:
  ₹7.69 crore.
- Grocery recorded the highest transaction volume:
  49,966 transactions.
- Education had the highest average transaction value:
  ₹5,094.02.
- Utilities contributed approximately ₹5.27 crore.
- Transport recorded the lowest average transaction value:
  ₹308.00.

Business takeaway:
UPI transactions show different usage patterns across merchant
categories. Grocery leads in transaction frequency, while
Shopping leads in total value and Education leads in average
transaction size.

### State-wise Transaction Analysis

- Maharashtra ranked first with 37,427 transactions.
- Maharashtra generated approximately ₹4.90 crore in transaction value.
- Uttar Pradesh ranked second, contributing 12.05% of transactions.
- Karnataka ranked third by transaction value.
- Rajasthan recorded the highest average transaction value
  at ₹1,337.79.
- The top three states (Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh and
  Karnataka) contributed 38.92% of total transactions.

Business takeaway:
Transaction activity is concentrated in the top three states
within this dataset, while average transaction values remain
relatively consistent across states.
